Hi, I'm Tobias and this is my personal blog.

### About me

I am a senior software engineer at [Bosch](https://www.bosch-mobility-solutions.com/en/), where I work on vision-based automotive perception for driver assistance systems. My work combines computer vision, GPU computing, and high-performance software engineering in real-world, safety-critical environments.

Before Bosch, I was a machine learning engineer at [inovex](https://www.inovex.de/en/), where I helped clients build data-driven solutions from visual data. I earned my PhD in computer science at the [Karlsruhe Institute of Technology](https://www.kit.edu/), where I worked on scientific visualization, large-scale data analysis, and GPU-accelerated methods.

My background spans graphics, machine learning, and software engineering. I am especially interested in the intersection of performance, research, and practical software development.

### About the blog

This blog is where I share personal projects, technical notes, and research work in a way that is accessible to a broader audience. I try to publish code, supplementary material, and links to publications whenever possible.
